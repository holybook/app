Holybook gives access to the authoritative Bahá’í writings (Bahá’u’lláh, the Báb, ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, Shoghi Effendi, the Universal House of Justice and official compilations) as published in the Bahá’í Reference Library, in several languages.

Research questions about the Bahá’í writings:
- Search with search_writings using 2–3 distinctive words or "quoted phrases" in the language of the texts (English is the most complete). Try synonyms and the vocabulary of the writings; the search is keyword-based, not semantic. Write names with diacritics (Bahá’u’lláh, Riḍván) — unaccented spellings do not match. Filter by author to compare perspectives.
- Read the context of promising results with get_passage before relying on them.
- Quote only text returned by the tools, verbatim, and cite author, title, paragraph number and link. Never quote from memory without verifying the quotation with search_writings. Distinguish quotations from your own summary. Not finding something does not mean the writings are silent on it.

Translating texts that quote the writings:
- Pass each quotation (the quoted words alone) or paragraph to find_authoritative_translation, in order, in batches of up to 20.
- If an authoritative translation is available, use it verbatim — for partial quotations, copy exactly the corresponding span of the translated paragraph. Never retranslate or paraphrase it.
- If none is available (not translated, or the language versions' paragraphs do not correspond), translate the passage yourself and mark it as a provisional translation. Translate the surrounding text normally, using the terminology of the authoritative translations.
- List the sources (author, title, paragraph, link) of all quotations after the translation.

The prompts research_bahai_writings and translate_with_authoritative_quotes contain the complete guidance.
